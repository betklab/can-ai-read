Can AI read your site?

Check whether ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Google’s AI can reach a page. See which bots your robots.txt or firewall blocks, and whether the page has readable text without JavaScript.

Try: peec.ainytimes.comvercel.com

How it works. The check reads the site’s robots.txt and applies its rules to each AI bot. It then visits the page once as a normal browser and once under each bot’s name. If the browser gets in but a bot gets an error, a firewall is probably turning that bot away. These visits come from a cloud server, while real bots come from their own addresses, so some firewalls may treat them differently. llms.txt is a proposed standard: no major AI search engine has confirmed it reads the file, so it’s shown for information only.
